Where I Started

(medicine → product → public health → founder)

I trained as a dentist. Five years of training, a gold medal at the end of it. A few months into practice, I realised I wasn't drawn to the chair in front of me but the system around it.

An MBA was the bridge. The decade since has been a deliberate move through every stage of company-building. On the way I learned the craft is the same at every stage. What changes is the constraint.

What I've Built

(ten years, four companies, sixteen countries)

Everwell Health Solutions

Senior PM to Principal PM · Bengaluru · 2020–2024

2.6M+Patients on Nikshay
16+Countries on Everwell Hub
5PMs & designers led and mentored

Owned two of Everwell's biggest bets in parallel: Nikshay, India's national TB platform serving 2.6 million patients, and the SaaS platform behind its international deployments across 16 countries.

The real lesson was about the people on the other side of the screen. Frontline health workers are overworked and stretched thin, and software built for them usually just duplicates their work. I learned to treat tech as an enabler, not the answer. I came from a world that optimised for the best mobile devices. At Everwell my users were often on feature phones, sitting in the remotest parts of the world where the infrastructure was often worse than what we could plan for.

What I'm Building Now

(my own bet)

HappiNest, maternal mental wellbeing

Founder & CEO · 2023–present · Bootstrapped, EBITA+

I started HappiNest after my own struggle with postpartum. Even with my medical training I couldn't recognise that what I was feeling had a name. The gap in maternal mental health isn't medication or information. It is a place to be heard, and a person to help you make sense of what you are feeling before you can name it yourself.

That is what HappiNest is. India's first maternal mental health platform, with a network of certified therapists, psychiatrists and coaches supporting families across the country.

Founding has been the sharpest teacher I have had. Building HappiNest from scratch has taught me more about users, unit economics and trade-offs than any roadmap ever could.
